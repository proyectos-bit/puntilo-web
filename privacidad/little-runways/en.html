Little Runways Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Little Runways is an Android game published by Puntilo Studio (package com.puntilo.littlerunways). This page explains which data is processed when you play, why, and who receives it.

Data that stays on your device

The game stores on your phone your progress (levels completed and stars), your coins and your settings. There is no user account and no cloud copy: this data never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which levels are hard, Little Runways uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It sends three notices per level attempt: started, won or lost, with the level number, whether it is a replay and how many rescues were used. The SDK adds its automatic session and first-open events, an app instance ID and an approximate location derived from the IP address. It does not send the advertising ID or your coins. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland analytics runs without an identifier: only notices without an app instance ID are sent. What the SDK collects: data collected by Google Analytics for Firebase. How Google handles data: Google Privacy Policy.

No ads

Little Runways does not show ads, includes no advertising SDK and does not use the Android advertising ID.

Children

Little Runways is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date. Questions: contacto@puntilo.com.
